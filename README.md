# Tic-Tac-Toe, Checkers, and Chess

A Safari-compatible browser game with selectable Tic-Tac-Toe, Checkers, Chess, Hangman, Sudoku, and Pig modes, shared difficulty settings, and a reset button.

## Pig (dice)

Roll the die to build a turn total, or hold to bank it. Rolling a 1 wipes the turn total and passes the die. First to 100 points wins.

You play against three computer players who take their turns one at a time, each with its own risk tolerance:

- **Jake** — conservative, banks early.
- **Jack** — fifty-fifty, coin-flips whether to keep pushing.
- **Lou** — absolutely crazy, keeps rolling far past sensible.

Pig has no difficulty setting; the computer players' risk tolerance provides the challenge, so the difficulty control is hidden in this mode.
